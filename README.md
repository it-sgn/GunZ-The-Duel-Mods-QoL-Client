## 📖 What Is GunZ: The Duel?

**GunZ: The Duel** is a third-person action shooter that combines stylish gunplay with acrobatic sword-fighting, originally developed by **MAIET Entertainment** in 2005 and brought back in a remastered version by **Masangsoft** for Steam in **August 2026** after 20 years . The game is **free to play** and quickly attracted over **2,200 concurrent players** on launch day, though it launched with only 41% positive reviews due to technical issues and AI-generated graphics .

The game is legendary for its **K‑Style** combat system — a complex set of animation-canceling techniques that allow players to perform insane combos like Butterfly, Slash Shot, and Reload Shot, creating a skill ceiling that has kept a dedicated community alive for decades .

**Key Game Features**:
- ⚔️ K‑Style action — wall-running, aerial combat, dashes, and weapon swapping
- 🎯 PvP and PvE modes — deathmatch, team deathmatch, berserker, quests, and boss fights
- 🎨 Deep character customization — permanent parts, weapon effects, and action effects
- 🏆 Clan wars and ranked matches with official tournaments

## 🎮 K‑Style Combat Guide (Legit Skills)

Mastering K‑Style is the key to dominating GunZ without cheats. These are legitimate techniques used by pro players.

### 🦋 Butterfly (BF)

A core K‑Style move that combines jumping, dashing, slashing, and blocking into a fluid motion.

**Input**: `[Jump] [Dash] [Slash] [Block]`

### 🦋 Double Butterfly (DBF)

An advanced version of Butterfly with an extra slash-block cycle.

**Input**: `[Jump] [Slash] [Block] [Dash] [Slash] [Block]`

### 🔫 Slash Shot (SS)

A technique that combines a sword slash with a quick gun shot. Switch to gun immediately after the slash, shoot, then switch back.

**Input**: `[Jump] [Dash] [Slash]` → switch to gun → `[Shoot]` → switch back to sword

### 🔄 Reload Shot (RS)

A technique that combines shooting with reloading while weapon swapping.

**Input**: `[Shoot]` → reload and switch to other gun at the same time → `[Shoot]`

### 🔀 Swap Shot

A pro move using two non-automatic guns (pistols or shotguns). Hold left mouse button on the first pistol, scroll to the next gun after the first shot, scroll back, and repeat until ammo runs out.

### 🗡️ Extend Lunge (Dagger)

A pro dagger move. Dash and before you see the lines, lunge.

**Input**: `W, W, Right Click`
